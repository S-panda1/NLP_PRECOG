The language of the book is quite simple and the explanations to the various scientific concepts are also quite lucid, some very interesting ideas about using higher dimensions and making AI as small as a proton (I believe the implications of this concept were kind of not discussed in depth in the book).
Had a lot of snippets of philosophy (existentialism) and commentary about politics, prose at some portions is quite good, but most of the times it quite flat. Good description of the life of trisolarians, kinda sad but universe can be a very harsh place, them existing is kind of a boon in the first place. Writing is mediocre at best, only one character had some kind of character development (ye wenjie), others seemed quite 1 dimensional, structure of the book is quite linear except some of the flashbacks, but they are handled well.
